# The 30-second recording

A script for one screen recording on an iPhone, for the top of the README. Five things in thirty seconds, in the order a person would meet them: the nearest open pharmacy with its walking time and ehditkö, a pharmacy's details, the medicine search, Riga with the counter card, and a visitor's report.

Thirty seconds holds this only if nothing on the way waits: no permission prompt, no typing that could have been done before, no loading. The preparation below takes those out.

## Before you record

**The moment.** Ehditkö shows only when the nearest open pharmacy closes within the hour. Record 20 to 50 minutes before a pharmacy near you closes. Open the app first: if the card has the line with two clock times ("Perillä", "Sulkeutuu"), the moment is right.

**The place.** The recording shows the map around you. Stand somewhere you are happy to show: a square in the centre, not your own street.

**The phone.**

1. Settings, Control Centre: add Screen Recording.
2. Turn a Focus on, so that no notification drops into the picture.
3. Dark or light, as you like; the dark theme shows the lit cross best after sunset.
4. Text size at the default, and the phone upright.

**Safari.**

1. Open <https://auki.vercel.app/fi>. Press "aA" (or the page menu) in the address bar, Website Settings, Location: Allow. No prompt will come during the recording.
2. Close every other tab.
3. Open the app once and let it find you, so that the tiles and the map are on the phone already.
4. Make a second tab for Riga: open the app in it, press the place name at the top, type "Riika", press "Hae", and wait for the results. Leave the tab open. A tab keeps its last search, so Riga will be there when you come back to it.
5. Go back to the first tab and reload it. It opens straight on the map, at the sheet's lowest place.

**The report.** A report sent from the live app is stored as a real one. Send it only if it is true: about a pharmacy you have just been to. Otherwise stop the recording with the chips pressed, before "Lähetä". The script below ends there either way.

**The medicine search.** No medicine needs to be named. The dialog with its empty field and the link "Avaa apteekki.fi" shows what the app does: it says where to check and sends nothing itself.

## The script

Start the recording from Control Centre, then switch to Safari. The seconds are where you should be when each step ends; cut the switch into Safari off the start afterwards.

| At | Do | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Nothing. Let the map stand. | The map, your dot, the lit crosses with their minutes, and the row: the nearest open pharmacy, its walking time, "Sulkeutuu pian" |
| 3–8 s | Pull the sheet up one place, to the card. Pause on it. | The walking time large, and ehditkö: when you would be there, when it closes, the minutes to spare |
| 8–13 s | Press the pharmacy's name. Then pull the sheet fully up. | The pharmacy's details: status and walking time, the row of buttons, the week's hours, the contacts |
| 13–17 s | Press "Lääkkeen saatavuus". Pause on the dialog. Close it with the cross. | Where to check a medicine's stock, the field, "Avaa apteekki.fi", and what is sent and what is not |
| 17–19 s | Switch to the Riga tab. | Riga, as it is at that hour |
| 19–25 s | Pull the sheet fully up and pause on "Resepti kotimaasta?". Press "Näytä apteekissa". Pause. | Whether a prescription from Finland works in Latvia, on the official pages' word, then the counter card in Latvian with the English under it |
| 25–26 s | Close the card with the cross. | |
| 26–30 s | Press the pharmacy's name, pull the sheet fully up and scroll to the end. Press "Kävitkö täällä?" and press two chips. Stop. | The report: one screen of chips, no text field |

Stop the recording from the red mark in the status bar.

## If thirty seconds is too tight in one take

Record three takes and join them: the first tab down to the medicine dialog (17 s), the Riga tab down to the counter card (8 s), and the report (5 s). The Photos app trims each; iMovie on the phone joins them. Cut on the moments where the screen stands still.

For the report alone, a pharmacy in the first tab is as good as one in Riga, and saves the scroll in a second city: open any row of the list, pull the sheet up and scroll to its end.

## After recording

1. Watch it once for anything that is not the app's: a notification, another tab's title, the time if it matters to you.
2. Trim the start and the end in Photos.
3. Put it at the top of the README, in place of the line that says it is to come. Dragging the file into a comment box on GitHub gives a link that plays in the README. GitHub takes videos of up to 10 MB that way on a free plan: if the recording is larger, export it smaller from iMovie first.
